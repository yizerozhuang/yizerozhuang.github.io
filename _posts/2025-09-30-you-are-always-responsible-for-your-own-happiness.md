No one is coming to you. After all, the journey of life depends on yourself alone. It's getting harder and harder to be happy comparing to the old time, when our sign widened to a certain extent, we started to compare ourself to different people in different stages, forgetting that life is more of a wilderness than a race.

About Nietzsche's idea of eternal recurrence [Eternal return](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_return#Friedrich_Nietzsche), I will be lying if I say I will be happy to restart my life again and again. My main reason is that the suffering of loneliness and trauma is almost unbearable despite the fact that there is happiness and joy in our lives as well, and I believe quite a lot of people will agree with my idea as well. So my result is that I would rather rest in peace eternal than go throught this again and again.
